# 02 — Market Analysis

## Direct Competitors (P2P Coupon Resale in India)

| Player | Status | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **No dominant player** | ✅ GAP | No scaled P2P coupon resale marketplace in India |
| Facebook Groups | Informal | Thousands of groups — proves demand |
| Telegram Channels | Informal | Active trading, zero trust |
| OLX / Quikr | Informal | People list gift cards, no flow |
| Reddit | Informal | Users swap/sell vouchers |

## Legal Risks

| Issue | Risk | Mitigation |
|-------|------|-----------|
| ToS "non-transferable" | 🔴 HIGH | Position as "gifting", start with gift cards |
| RBI PPI guidelines | 🟡 MEDIUM | Legal opinion, monitor regulations |
| GST | 🟡 MEDIUM | GST on commission |
| Fraud/stolen codes | 🔴 HIGH | KYC + escrow + trust scoring |
