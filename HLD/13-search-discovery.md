# 13 — Search & Discovery

## Search Engine
Meilisearch (Phase 1) or Elasticsearch (Phase 2+)
- Full-text search with typo tolerance
- Filters: brand, category, price, discount %, expiry, seller tier, verified
- Sort: best discount, newest, ending soon, seller rating

## Home Screen Feeds

| Feed | Logic | Purpose |
|------|-------|---------|
| 🔥 Trending | Most bought in 24 hours | Social proof |
| ⏰ Expiring Soon | Expiring within 7 days | Urgency |
| 💰 Best Discounts | Highest % off | Value seekers |
| 🛡️ Guaranteed | Platform-verified | Trust-conscious |
| 🆕 Just Listed | Newest listings | Fresh inventory |
| ❤️ For You | Based on past purchases | Personalization (Phase 2) |
